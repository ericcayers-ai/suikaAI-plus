Bundled fruit PBR textures
==========================

Source: TextureCan (https://www.texturecan.com), "Food" texture category.

Downloaded 2026-07-02, 1K resolution, for the Realistic Textures mode
(RayTraceLab / PbrFruitMaterials). Each folder holds albedo.jpg, normal.png
(OpenGL/+Y tangent-space convention), roughness.jpg, ao.jpg, height.png.

Mapping to in-game fruit tiers (chosen for closest visual/botanical match —
this game has no exact texture for every tier, so several tiers below fall
back to the procedural PBR generator instead of a photo-sourced texture):

  watermelon/  -> tier 11 WATERMELON (exact match)
  apple/       -> tier 6  APPLE      (exact match)
  strawberry/  -> tier 2  STRAWBERRY (exact match)
  grape/       -> tier 3  GRAPE      (sourced as TextureCan's "Blackberry" —
                                       closest available small dark berry skin)
  dekopon/     -> tier 4  DEKOPON    (sourced as TextureCan's "Orange Peel" —
                                       dekopon is itself a citrus, genuine match)

Tiers 1 (cherry), 5 (persimmon), 7 (pear), 8 (peach), 9 (pineapple), and
10 (melon) have no bundled photo texture and use the procedural PBR
generator (see PbrFruitMaterials.java) instead.
