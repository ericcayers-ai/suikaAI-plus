  table_wood/  -> "Pine Wood (Wood 0054)", https://www.texturecan.com/details/535/
                  Used as the table surface the glass jar sits on.
                  albedo.jpg, normal.png (OpenGL/+Y convention),
                  roughness.jpg, ao.jpg.

  studio.hdr   -> "Pav Studio 03" HDRI, https://polyhaven.com/a/pav_studio_03
                  Source: Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0 Universal
                  ("All assets on this site are CC0... free for any purpose").
                  1K equirectangular Radiance (.hdr). Drives the RT Lab's
                  image-based environment lighting: sky/ambient fill, the glass
                  jar's reflections, and the metal chute's mirror reflections.

The tan wall behind the table is a procedural material (flat tan albedo with
subtle noise) tuned to sit tonally inside the HDRI, not a downloaded texture.
